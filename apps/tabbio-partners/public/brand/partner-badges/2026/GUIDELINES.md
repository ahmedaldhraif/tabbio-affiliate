# Tabbio Active Partner 2026 Badge Guidelines

Version: 2026.2 approved artwork system  
Applies to: `Tabbio Active Partner 2026` stacked and horizontal badges

## 1. Purpose

The Tabbio Active Partner badge identifies a person or organization with active access to the Tabbio Partner Program during 2026. It is a program-status mark.

It does not certify professional skill, guarantee results or income, create an employment or agency relationship, appoint an official Tabbio representative, or endorse the partner's separate services.

## 2. Approved badges

There are two approved compositions. They carry the same meaning.

### Stacked badge

Use the stacked badge when the credential should stand on its own:

- LinkedIn Featured media
- Portfolio or profile credential section
- Media kit or one-sheet
- Proposal cover or partner announcement
- Social post or story artwork

Master aspect ratio: `942 × 526` (approximately `1.79:1`).

### Horizontal badge

Use the horizontal badge when vertical space is limited:

- Website partnership or credentials section
- Email signature
- CV, résumé, or portfolio footer
- Presentation footer
- Proposal, document, or media-kit footer

Master aspect ratio: `1658 × 303` (approximately `5.47:1`).

Do not recreate one composition from the other. Use the supplied file that fits the placement.

## 3. File selection

| File         | Use                                                                                |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SVG master   | Websites, design tools, professional print, and any scalable placement             |
| PNG          | Documents, email signatures, office software, and platforms that do not accept SVG |
| PNG `@2x`    | High-density screens or exports that require extra raster resolution               |
| WebP preview | Tabbio product previews only; not the distributed master                           |

Never screenshot the badge or download it from a social post. Start from the current Partner Resources kit.

## 4. Clear space

Keep empty space around every side equal to at least half the badge's height. No text, logo, photograph, border, or interface control may enter this area.

This conservative spacing keeps the badge independent from the partner's own identity and follows established partner-badge practice.

## 5. Minimum size

Maintain legibility of `Active Partner 2026`.

| Badge      | Digital minimum | Print minimum |
| ---------- | --------------: | ------------: |
| Stacked    |     240 px wide |    45 mm wide |
| Horizontal |     420 px wide |    75 mm wide |

If the status line is difficult to read, use a larger size or a different placement. Do not remove or enlarge only the status line.

## 6. Background and contrast

The approved badge includes its own violet surface and white artwork. Place it on a quiet background where its complete rounded silhouette remains visible.

Preferred backgrounds:

- White or warm neutral
- Foundation black or charcoal
- A single low-detail brand surface

Avoid photographs, strong patterns, competing gradients, low-contrast violet fields, and any background that makes the edge disappear. Do not place another panel, border, glow, or shadow behind the badge to force contrast; choose a better placement.

## 7. Placement hierarchy

The partner's own name or brand must remain primary. The Tabbio badge should support identity, not replace it.

- Keep the badge smaller and less prominent than the partner's own logo or name.
- Separate it from the partner logo by the required clear space.
- Do not create a combined or co-branded logo lockup.
- Do not use it as an avatar, account name, social handle, favicon, app icon, or profile picture.
- Do not place it in a navigation header in a way that suggests Tabbio operates the partner's website.

## 8. Approved uses

- Show current participation in the Tabbio Partner Program.
- Add it to a website credentials or partnerships section.
- Add it to a CV, résumé, portfolio, proposal, presentation, or media kit.
- Add it to an email signature when the status line remains legible.
- Share it in a partner announcement with an accurate relationship statement.
- Link a digital badge to the partner's public Tabbio status page when that service becomes available.

## 9. Prohibited changes

Use the artwork exactly as supplied. Do not:

- Redraw, trace, imitate, or typeset any part
- Crop, stretch, squash, rotate, skew, mirror, or animate it
- Change the gradient, color, opacity, texture, corner shape, spacing, or proportions
- Remove, rewrite, translate, or reposition `Active Partner 2026`
- Add a specialty, tier, award, country, service, or partner name inside the badge
- Add another logo, icon, seal, flag, QR code, outline, border, shadow, glow, or effect
- Extract the Tabbio wordmark from the badge for unrelated use
- Place the badge inside the partner's own logo or product identity
- Use an expired file or continue displaying it after partner access ends

## 10. Relationship language

Approved examples:

> We participate in the Tabbio Partner Program.

> We may earn a commission when someone subscribes through our Tabbio partner link.

> Tabbio and our business are separate. Our service fees and client relationship remain our own.

Do not say or imply:

- Tabbio-certified career coach, recruiter, creator, or agency
- Official Tabbio recruiter, representative, office, or employee
- Approved, preferred, exclusive, best, or number-one Tabbio provider
- Tabbio guarantees our services, hiring outcomes, job offers, earnings, or results
- The badge verifies a person's professional qualifications

## 11. Disclosure

The badge never replaces an affiliate or advertising disclosure. Place a clear disclosure close to every tracked link or promotional recommendation.

Recommended disclosure:

> I may earn a commission if you subscribe through this link.

Partners remain responsible for the laws and platform rules that apply to their location, audience, content, and advertising activity.

## 12. Accessibility

- Use alternative text: `Tabbio Active Partner 2026`.
- Do not repeat visible adjacent text in the alternative text.
- Do not place instructions, eligibility, verification details, or commission disclosures only inside the image.
- Preserve the SVG title and accessible name when embedding the asset directly.
- Keep the badge large enough for the status line to remain readable at 200% zoom.

## 13. Status, validity, and removal

- Tabbio issues the badge only after partner approval.
- The 2026 artwork is valid only while the partner remains active during 2026.
- Stop displaying it immediately when access expires, is suspended, revoked, or terminated.
- Replace it when Tabbio publishes a new annual kit. Do not edit the year yourself.
- A downloaded file alone is not proof of current status.
- A future public partner-status page should be the verification source for digital use. That service is not connected in this frontend prototype.

## 14. Ownership and enforcement

Tabbio retains ownership of the badge, wordmark, and related brand assets. Permission is limited, revocable, non-transferable, and tied to active program status. Tabbio may require correction or removal of misleading, modified, expired, unlawful, or otherwise non-compliant use.

This guideline is a product and brand-policy draft. Final trademark, program-terms, enforcement, territory, and legal language require Tabbio legal approval before production launch.
