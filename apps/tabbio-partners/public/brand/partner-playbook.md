# Tabbio Partner Playbook: Prototype

Version: 2026-08-09

## Lead with useful work

Show how a live CV link helps a real audience. Use a practical example, an honest claim, and a clear next step.

## Disclose the partnership

Place this near the tracked link: “I may earn a commission if you subscribe through this link.”

## Use real claims

Only describe Tabbio capabilities or outcomes you can verify. Do not promise earnings or candidate results.

## Respect protected terms

Do not buy ads on protected Tabbio brand terms unless the program terms explicitly allow it.

## Check local rules

Confirm advertising permits and disclosure rules for every place where your content appears.

## Use your Partner credential carefully

After approval, use only the credential edition assigned to your partner type. Choose the horizontal version for websites and documents, the compact version for email and footers, and the social version for posts or Featured media. Keep your own identity primary. Do not use the credential as your avatar, alter its role or active year, merge it into your logo, or imply that you work for or are certified by Tabbio. The credential never replaces a commission disclosure.

Replace annual files when Tabbio publishes a new active-year kit. Production digital credentials should link to the partner's public status page. See `partner-badge-guidelines.md` for the complete prototype policy.

This file is a prototype resource, not approved legal guidance.
